## Effort Scale
We use T-shirt sizes for relative effort estimation:
- **S**: Small (minor fixes, already done, or very simple tasks)
- **M**: Medium (some missing features, requires focused effort)
- **L**: Large (significant missing functionality, complex tasks)
- **XL**: Extra Large (massive gaps, requires major effort)

## AI Ease Scale
We estimate how easy it might be for AI-assisted development to quickly achieve compliance:
- **High**: AI can likely solve this with minimal guidance (e.g., pattern-based fixes, simple method implementations).
- **Medium**: AI can solve this if guided with specific patterns or examples (e.g., mapping options, standard SQL translations).
- **Low**: Requires deep architectural understanding, complex reasoning, or complex Calcite rules that AI typically struggles with without heavy human oversight.
